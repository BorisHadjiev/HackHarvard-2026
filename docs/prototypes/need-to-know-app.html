A narrow question, answered privately.

Need-to-Know verifies one thing: whether a tenant’s income meets a rent requirement. Here is exactly where the line sits.

1

Only a yes or no leaves the device.

The tenant receives a link scoped to one request and one landlord. They run the check on their own device, then choose whether to share the result.

2

No financial paperwork changes hands.

Income amounts, balances, transactions, statements, and bank credentials stay on the tenant’s device. The landlord sees an eligibility result and verification metadata — never the underlying records.

3

No tenant profile follows them around.

There is no global tenant identifier and no cross-landlord comparison. A result belongs to this request and this landlord only.

4

The landlord learns the bar was met, not by how much.

Because the question is “at least 3× rent,” a yes implies income above that threshold. The exact amount is never disclosed.

Prototype disclosure This demo uses synthetic data. The private link is a demo route; the on-device check and signature are simulated for illustration. It is not a zero-knowledge proof, a production audit, or a certified verification service.
